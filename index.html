FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before starting your interior project.

How long have you been in the interior design business? +
Right Choice Creators Interiors has been in the business for over 16 years since 2008, led by P. Rajesh Kumar DCA. We have completed 500+ projects across Tamil Nadu including modular kitchens, bedroom interiors, and complete home packages.
Which areas and cities do you serve? +
We primarily serve Dindigul (our home base) and deliver across Tamil Nadu — Chennai, Coimbatore, Tiruppur, Trichy (Tiruchirappalli), and Madurai. We also serve select projects in Bangalore. Call 99761 85002 to confirm availability in your area.
What services do you offer? +
We offer Modular Kitchens (L-shape, U-shape, island), Bedroom Interiors (wardrobes, bed panels, dressing units), TV Units & Living Room Furniture, Office & Commercial Furniture, Pooja & Study Room Woodwork, and Complete Home Interior packages from concept to completion.
How long does a modular kitchen installation take? +
Depending on scope and size, a modular kitchen typically takes 7–21 working days from measurement to final installation. A complete home interior project may take 45–90 days. We discuss and commit to a timeline upfront before starting any work.
Do you provide a warranty on your work? +
Yes. We stand behind our craftsmanship and materials. We use only premium-grade hardware and boards — Hettich, Häfele, BWR plywood, branded laminates — ensuring lasting quality. Warranty terms vary by material and scope; we discuss these clearly before starting your project.
How do I get a free quote? +
Fill the enquiry form on this page or WhatsApp us directly at 99761 85002. Share your requirements — room size, style preference, budget range — and we will reply within 24 hours with a detailed estimate. Site visits are free for Dindigul and nearby areas.
Can I visit your studio to see material samples? +
Absolutely! We welcome you to visit our studio at Main Road, Veerasikkampattai, Bodikamanvadi, Dindigul – 624708. You can touch and feel our material samples, finish options, and hardware quality firsthand. We are open all days — no weekly holiday. Call ahead to schedule a convenient time.
